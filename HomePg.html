Home

Meet Aryan — a first-year student who's a little lost. He's not sure which course to pick, what lab equipment he actually needs, and his budget is already tight. College is overwhelming enough on its own, and on top of that, everything is scattered across different places — haggling with strangers on OLX, hunting for notes in random WhatsApp groups, or trying to track down a senior just to get his doubts cleared.

Now imagine Aryan finds one platform where he can rent a lab coat and drafter from a verified senior on his own campus, grab free notes from the E-library, book a mentor to clear his doubts, and compare courses to pick the right one — all in one place, with zero stress. That's exactly what we're building: a way to make college life simpler, more affordable, and all in one spot.



Technical Stack


The development plan for this project utilizes a structured technical stack to integrate frontend design with backend data management:

  • Frontend Architecture: The home page is developed using HTML and CSS, incorporating the Tailwind CSS framework to build a responsive, utility-driven user interface.

  • Database Management: A MySQL relational database will be developed for backend storage. Once built, it is designed to manage student authentication credentials (usernames and passwords). The intended database schema will also scale to store comprehensive student and user records, material datasets, and e-library data.

  • System Integration: JDBC (Java Database Connectivity) will be implemented to establish the connection between the frontend web application and the MySQL database, enabling secure and reliable data transactions.
