About Us

Who We Are

We're a group of college students who got tired of two things: paying full price for gear we'd use for one semester, and struggling to find the right guidance or course without wading through a hundred confusing options online. So we built a platform that solves both — a place where students can buy, sell, rent, and learn, all within a trusted campus community.

We started as two ideas — a peer-to-peer marketplace and a skill-building hub — and realized students needed both under one roof. That's how this platform came to be.

Our Motto

"Share. Learn. Save."

We believe college shouldn't mean overspending on things you'll use once, or navigating your degree without guidance from people who've already been there.

Why We Should Be Trusted

  • Every user joins with a verified college ID — no outside strangers, no fake accounts
  • Transactions and mentorship happen within your own trusted campus circle
  • Ratings and reviews keep every listing and mentor accountable
  • We're students too — we use this platform exactly like you will

What the Site Currently Provides

  • A free E-library of resources and notes
  • A peer-to-peer marketplace for academic gear and equipment
  • A course comparison tool to help you choose wisely
  • Hourly mentorship sessions with seniors and mentors
  • A digital community space
  • Account management for tracking your listings, sessions, and history

Our Future Plans/Prospects

  • Expanding to more colleges beyond our pilot campus
  • Adding AI-powered matching for mentors and course recommendations
  • Introducing paid seminars and skill workshops
  • Growing a stronger peer/mentor network campus by campus
